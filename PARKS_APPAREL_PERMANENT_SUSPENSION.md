## The Honest Question: Is This the Real Problem?

Before you create a new account, you need to understand:

**What actually got you suspended?**

Common misrepresentation issues for outdoor apparel:
1. **National Parks Claims**
   - False: "Official National Parks apparel" (you're not endorsed)
   - False: "Officially supports the NPS" (unless you donate)
   - False: Implying NPS affiliation without it

2. **Origin Claims**
   - False: "Made in USA" (if manufactured overseas)
   - False: "American made" (if not entirely US-made)

3. **Environmental Claims**
   - False: "Eco-friendly" (without certification)
   - False: "Sustainable" (vague, unsubstantiated)
   - False: "Organic cotton" (without proof)

4. **Quality Claims**
   - False: "Premium quality" (subjective, unprovable)
   - False: "Best on the market" (unsubstantiated)
   - False: "Professional grade" (for consumer products)

5. **Availability Issues**
   - False: Product "In Stock" when it's not
   - False: Shipping time claims you can't meet
