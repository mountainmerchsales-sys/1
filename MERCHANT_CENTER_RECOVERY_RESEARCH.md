## The New Account Trap

### ❌ Why Creating a New Account FAILS

**Google's Detection System:**
- Google's systems **automatically flag new accounts** created by suspended business entities
- If the new account uses the same:
  - Business name
  - Domain
  - Email address
  - Product catalog
  - Bank account
  - Shipping address
  
...Google will **suspend it again** (often faster than the first time)

**Worse: Circumvention Penalty**
- Google may classify it as "**Circumventing Systems**"
- This triggers a **HARDER suspension** more difficult to appeal
- Timeline: 7-30 days to ban the new account too

**Evidence from Research:**
> "Do not create a new Merchant Center account to bypass suspension — that's likely to get the new account suspended too."
> 
> "If you create a new Merchant Center account for the same business as a suspended account, the new account is likely to also be suspended."

## ✅ What ACTUALLY Works

### The Correct Path: Fix & Appeal

**Success Rate:** 60-80% of accounts get reinstated if issues are properly fixed

**Timeline:** 7-14 business days (vs. months of trying new accounts)

### The Process:

**Step 1: Identify Root Cause** (1-2 days)
- Check Google Merchant Center notifications
- Review suspension email details
- Identify SPECIFIC policy violated
- Common issues:
  - Price mismatches (site ≠ feed)
  - Missing/incorrect product info
  - Misleading promotions
  - Shipping/return policy issues
  - Contact information problems
  - Product availability false claims

**Step 2: Fix Everything** (3-5 days)
- Update website product info
- Fix data feed attributes
- Correct prices, shipping, availability
- Verify all product details match across:
  - ✅ Website
  - ✅ Merchant Center
  - ✅ Data feed
  - ✅ Inventory system
- Update business policies
- Ensure accurate contact information

**Step 3: Test** (1-2 days)
- Use Google Merchant Center's testing tools
- Validate feed quality
- Check for warnings
- Preview product listings

**Step 4: Submit Appeal** (1 day)
- **CRITICAL:** Only appeal AFTER fixing everything
- Submit detailed explanation of fixes
- Provide evidence of changes
- Be honest and specific

**Step 5: Wait for Review** (7-10 business days)
- Google reviews your fixes
- If complete, account reinstated
- If not, you get feedback to fix more
