# Parks Apparel — Meta Ads Strategy (Relaunch)

**Store:** parksapparel.com  
**Budget:** Under $500/month  
**Goal:** Drive first sales (purchase conversions)  
**Platform:** Meta (Facebook + Instagram)  
**Season:** Summer relaunch — peak national parks travel season (June–August)

---

## Step 1: Reactivate These Products First

Focus ads on 3–5 hero products before running a full catalog. These have the broadest appeal:

| Priority | Product | Why |
|----------|---------|-----|
| 1 | Yosemite-themed T-shirt (unisex) | Most recognized park in the US |
| 2 | Arches NP T-shirt or mug | Instagram-favorite landscape, very visual |
| 3 | Grand Canyon mug (11oz) | Low price point ($9.95) = easy impulse buy |
| 4 | Grand Teton or Glacier women's tee | Strong female buyer demo for parks apparel |
| 5 | Pacific Crest Trail tee | Dedicated fanbase, high-intent niche |

---

## Step 2: Campaign Structure

### Budget Allocation (~$400/month on spend, ~$13/day)

```
1 Campaign: Sales (Purchase conversion event)
│
├── Ad Set 1: Outdoor/Hiking Interests — $7/day (~$210/mo)
│   ├── Ad A: Lifestyle photo — Yosemite tee
│   ├── Ad B: Lifestyle photo — Arches tee
│   └── Ad C: Product video or carousel — mugs
│
└── Ad Set 2: National Parks Fans — $6/day (~$180/mo)
    ├── Ad A: Same as Ad Set 1 Ad A (let Meta optimize)
    ├── Ad B: Gift angle copy variant
    └── Ad C: Carousel of top 3 products
```

**Hold $50–80/month in reserve** for boosting a winning ad once you identify it (week 3–4).

## Step 3: Audience Targeting

### Ad Set 1 — Outdoor/Hiking Interests
- **Location:** United States
- **Age:** 28–55
- **Interests (stack these):**
  - Hiking
  - Camping
  - REI (brand)
  - Patagonia (brand)
  - National Geographic
  - Outdoor Recreation
  - Trail running
- **Exclude:** People who already purchased (pixel-based, once pixel has data)
- **Placement:** Instagram Feed, Instagram Reels, Facebook Feed

### Ad Set 2 — National Parks Fans
- **Location:** United States
- **Age:** 25–60
- **Interests:**
  - National Park Service
  - Yosemite National Park
  - Grand Canyon
  - Hiking trails
  - America's National Parks
  - Leave No Trace
- **Behaviors:** Frequent travelers, engaged shoppers
- **Placement:** Instagram Feed, Facebook Feed

> **Tip:** Keep audiences broad at first (1M–5M reach). With a small budget, narrow audiences exhaust too fast and exit the learning phase slowly.

## Step 5: Creative Recommendations

### What to Shoot/Use
1. **Lifestyle over product shots** — person wearing tee outdoors, on a trail, at a park
2. **Mug lifestyle** — mug on a wooden table, near a campfire, morning light
3. **UGC style** — casual phone-shot feel outperforms polished studio work on Meta
4. **Aspect ratios:** 4:5 for Feed, 9:16 for Reels/Stories

### Quick Creative Wins (no photoshoot needed)
- Use Canva to place product mockups over park background photos
- Add text overlay with park name + price
- Short Reel: slideshow of 5–6 park tees with trending audio

---

## Step 6: Pixel & Tracking Setup (Do First)

Before spending a dollar on ads, make sure:

- [ ] Meta Pixel installed on Shopify (via Meta channel app)
- [ ] Purchase, Add to Cart, and View Content events firing
- [ ] Conversions API connected (boosts tracking accuracy ~20–30%)
- [ ] UTM parameters on all ad links (`?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=relaunch`)

---

## Step 7: Week-by-Week Launch Plan

| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| Week 1 | Reactivate 3–5 hero products, set up pixel, launch 2 ad sets |
| Week 2 | Monitor CPM, CTR, and add-to-cart rate. Don't change anything yet |
| Week 3 | Kill underperforming ads (CTR < 0.8%). Double down on winner |
| Week 4 | Use reserve budget ($50–80) to boost the best-performing ad |
| Month 2 | Add retargeting ad set for store visitors (if pixel has 500+ events) |

---

## Key Metrics to Watch

| Metric | Target (starting out) |
|--------|-----------------------|
| CPM | $10–$20 |
| CTR (link) | > 1% |
| CPC | < $1.50 |
| Add-to-Cart Rate | > 2% of link clicks |
| ROAS | > 2x by month 2 |
