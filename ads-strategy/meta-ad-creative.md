### 1. Protect Our Parks Big Bend T-Shirt
**Price:** $29.95 | **URL:** parksapparel.com/products/protect-our-parks-big-bend-national-park-unisex-fit-soft-t-shirt

**Why this works on Meta:** The "Protect Our Parks" framing activates cause-driven buyers — a huge segment on Instagram/Facebook. Stops the scroll with purpose, not just product.

#### Primary Text (3 versions — test all)

**Version A — Cause angle:**
```
800,000 acres of Chihuahuan Desert. Rugged canyons. The Rio Grande.

Big Bend is one of America's most remote and least-visited national parks — and it deserves more protection.

Our Protect Our Parks tee celebrates the wild beauty of Big Bend and the fight to keep it wild.

🌵 Shop now → parksapparel.com
```

**Version B — Identity angle:**
```
You've been to the parks most people only see in photos.

The Protect Our Parks Big Bend Tee is for people who actually show up.

Soft unisex fit. Available in sizes S–3XL.

👇 Get yours before it sells out.
```

**Version C — Short punchy (best for Reels/Stories):**
```
Big Bend. 800,000 acres. Zero crowds.

Protect what you love. 🌵

Shop Parks Apparel → parksapparel.com
```

#### Headlines (for carousel or single image):
- "Protect What You Love"
- "Big Bend Deserves Better"
- "For People Who Show Up"
- "America's Most Wild & Remote Park"

#### Creative Direction:
- Use the product image on a warm desert/canyon background
- Text overlay: "Protect Our Parks" in a bold serif or distressed font
- Color palette: terracotta, sand, deep sage
- Video idea: timelapse or drone-style Big Bend footage with tee shown at end

## Advantage+ Campaign: How to Use These Creatives

### Upload Strategy
- Upload all 3 copy versions per product as separate ads in your ASC
- Meta will automatically test them and push budget to the winner
- Don't kill an ad in the first 7 days — let the algorithm learn

### Recommended Ad Format Per Product
| Product | Best Format |
|---------|------------|
| Big Bend "Protect Our Parks" | Single image + Reel (cause content) |
| Yellowstone Trucker Cap | Carousel (show all colorways) |
| Angels Landing Zion Tee | Single image (iconic visual) |
| Big Bend Sunrise Tee | Single image |
| Yosemite Mug | Carousel (4 colors) or Collection ad |

### Carousel Idea: The Big Bend Collection
Run a single carousel that shows both Big Bend shirts together:
- Card 1: Protect Our Parks tee ($29.95)
- Card 2: Sunrise River tee ($29.95)
- Card 3: Shop the collection CTA → parksapparel.com

### Low-Budget Sequencing
With under $500/month, don't run all 5 at once. Suggested order:

**Week 1–2:** Big Bend Sunrise River (proven demand, 11 orders)
**Week 3–4:** Add Yosemite Mug (low price = more conversions = more pixel data)
**Month 2:** Add Protect Our Parks + Yellowstone Cap once pixel has data
**Month 2:** Reactivate Angels Landing and add to campaign
