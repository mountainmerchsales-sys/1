## 1. Brand Voice & Tone (Canva: "Brand Voice")

**One-line summary:** Premium apparel inspired by America's national parks and the outdoors — for people who belong on the trails.

**Voice traits:**
- **Evocative & sensory** — lead with the place, not the product. ("Old Faithful, bison-filled valleys, and the world's highest concentration of geysers…")
- **Specific & knowledgeable** — real park facts and landmarks build trust (acreage, named trails, geology, wildlife).
- **Premium but approachable** — "heavyweight ring-spun cotton," "garment-dyed," "lived-in feel" — quality without snobbery.
- **Identity-driven** — speak to who the customer *is*: hikers, campers, road-trippers, park lovers, gift-givers.
- **Warm, never corporate** — campfire energy, not catalog copy.

**Sentence rhythm that works for you:** Short punchy opener → specific sensory detail → product payoff.
> *"No cell service. No crowds. Just canyon, river, and sky. Big Bend National Park Sunrise River Tee."*

**Do say:** wild, iconic, trailhead, road trip, lived-in, ring-spun, adventure-ready, the parks you love
**Avoid:** generic hype ("amazing deal!!"), clickbait, anything that doesn't sound like it came from someone who's actually been to the park.

**Alt pairing — "Premium Vintage"**
